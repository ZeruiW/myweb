## Research Focus

My work focuses on making AI systems more **transparent**, **trustworthy**, and **secure**:

### Explainable AI (XAI)
Developing methods to interpret complex AI model decisions, enabling humans to understand and trust AI systems. My XAIport framework (ICSE 2024) enables early XAI adoption in MLOps pipelines.

### Video Understanding
Building transformer-based models for real-time video analysis. STAA (IEEE Access 2025) provides spatio-temporal explanations with sub-150ms latency.

### AI Security
Investigating adversarial attacks and defenses for video models. My work on joint spatio-temporal attacks (ACM TOMM 2025) reveals critical vulnerabilities in video transformers.

### Cloud AI Services
Creating frameworks for deploying XAI in production environments. The Cloud XAI architecture (IEEE TCC 2024) enables trustworthy explanations for Azure, GCP, and AWS services.
